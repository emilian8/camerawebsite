Meet Leilani

Capturing love, one frame at a time.

I'm Leilani Camacho, a wedding and portrait photographer based in Palm Springs and Los Angeles. My approach is calm, collaborative, and focused on helping you feel at ease so the real connection shows up in every frame.

From elopements under desert skies to seaside celebrations, I blend candid moments with light direction to create galleries that feel effortless and elevated - images you'll return to for years.
